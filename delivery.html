Good to know

Delivery details

Order timing

Order before 4:00 PM the day before your scheduled delivery. Orders after the cut-off go to the next available slot.

Delivery areas

We deliver across Hong Kong Island, Kowloon and the New Territories. Restricted zones, remote areas and outlying islands are not served. Eligibility is confirmed at checkout.

Upper floor charges

For buildings without lift access, HK$30 per floor above ground level applies. Ground floor collection is recommended.

Delivery & liability

Deliveries are made on designated days. If you ask for goods to be left unattended, we can’t accept liability for loss, theft, damage or spoilage.

Weather delays

Deliveries may be delayed or cancelled under Typhoon Signal No. 8 or above, or a Black Rainstorm Warning. We’ll notify you and reschedule promptly.

Our team can confirm delivery eligibility for your address. Contact us →
